What I Do

Statistical & ML Modeling

Classification, forecasting and NLP with scikit-learn, XGBoost and LightGBM, validated with k-fold CV and ROC-AUC against a real baseline. Hypothesis testing, ANOVA, Chi-Square and Mann-Whitney U when the question is whether an effect is real.

Lakehouse & Pipelines
